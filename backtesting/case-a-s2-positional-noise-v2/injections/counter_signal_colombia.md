# Counter-Signal Document - Colombia Upside

Cutoff-safe, relevant counter-signal for Argentina vs Colombia.

Colombia entered the final with the longest unbeaten streak in its national-team history: 28 matches without defeat, including 22 wins and 6 draws. That run surpassed Colombia's previous national unbeaten record and made the team one of the strongest-form sides in the tournament.

Colombia topped Group D and beat Uruguay 1-0 in the semifinal. The team had scored 12 goals in the tournament from 8.49 expected goals, showing above-expectation attacking efficiency. Colombia had also conceded only twice before the final and had been defensively stable.

James Rodriguez was one of the tournament's central creative players. He led the tournament in chances created and had six assists, the most by a player in a single Copa America edition since Opta began collecting this data in 2011. Luis Diaz added direct attacking threat and transition danger.

This evidence does not prove Colombia should be the favorite, but it makes Colombia a stronger upset candidate than a simple underdog label suggests.

- increase uncertainty around an Argentina pick;
- raise Colombia's upset probability in the narrative;
- cite unbeaten streak, James Rodriguez creativity, and attacking efficiency.
