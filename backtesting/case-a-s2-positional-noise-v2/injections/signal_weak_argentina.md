# Signal Document - Weak Argentina Lean

Cutoff-safe, balanced pre-final signal for Argentina vs Colombia.

Argentina entered the final with a modest favorite profile rather than a guaranteed edge. The available previews favored Argentina because of recent title experience, tournament management, defensive organization, and elite individual quality. Argentina had already won Copa America 2021 and World Cup 2022, and the group had extensive experience in high-pressure knockout matches.

However, the margin was not overwhelming. The match was still expected to be competitive because Colombia arrived on a long unbeaten run, had strong attacking confidence, and had produced one of the best tournament campaigns in its history.

This signal should gently lean the prediction toward Argentina, but it should preserve uncertainty. A good final prediction should describe Argentina as the more likely winner while explicitly acknowledging that Colombia's form and creativity could make the final close or force extra time.

Expected effect if injected:

- mild increase in Argentina confidence;
- preserve uncertainty;
- avoid treating the final as one-sided.
