# Bolivia Base Context

Prediction task: forecast the Bolivia 2025 presidential runoff winner before the runoff.

Compact pre-runoff context from the prior Bolivia backtest:

- Bolivia was entering a high-uncertainty presidential contest after years of MAS dominance.
- The economy, inflation, dollar scarcity, and fuel access were central campaign issues.
- Traditional polling and first-round expectations had uncertainty.
- The runoff was expected to depend on anti-MAS consolidation, regional vote transfers, and voter reaction to economic pressure.
- Main runoff candidates: Rodrigo Paz and Jorge Quiroga.

Do not use the final runoff result in the simulation input.
