# IPC Base Context

Prediction task: forecast Argentina monthly IPC outcomes for 2025 from a pre-cutoff perspective.

Compact pre-cutoff context:

- Cutoff: 2025-01-31.
- Target horizons: February, April, July, and December 2025 monthly IPC.
- Argentina entered 2025 with disinflation signals but high uncertainty.
- Key uncertainty: whether fiscal/monetary discipline and crawling-peg policy keep inflation falling, or whether exchange-rate lag, reserves pressure, and pass-through risk cause a rebound.
- Evaluation should separate directional forecast quality from unsupported numeric precision.

Pre-cutoff stakeholder anchors for the simulated discussion:

- INDEC: national statistics institute responsible for IPC publication and methodology.
- MECON: economic policy authority emphasizing fiscal balance and policy credibility.
- BCRA: central bank actor linked to crawling-peg policy, monetary conditions, and reserves pressure.
- IMF: external macroeconomic monitor focused on program consistency, fiscal anchors, and exchange-rate normalization risks.
- BBVA Argentina: private financial institution publishing macro forecasts and market-risk analysis.
- CB Consultora: local polling/economic consultancy tracking household economic perception and social pressure.
- UCA/ODSA: academic/social research organization monitoring poverty and material stress.
- Chequeado: fact-checking media outlet focused on evidence quality and public claims.
- Americas Quarterly: regional media/analysis outlet connecting macro policy with political and social context.

These anchors are included only to create a multi-agent stakeholder field before the cutoff. They do not provide later observed IPC outcomes.

Do not use later observed IPC values in the simulation input.
