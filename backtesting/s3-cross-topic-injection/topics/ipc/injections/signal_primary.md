# Signal: Disinflation Track

Evidence favoring continued disinflation:

- The REM/BCRA-style outlook pointed to lower expected inflation and a more stable nominal path.
- BBVA-style outlook discussed crawling-peg changes and projected lower annual inflation relative to prior crisis levels.
- Fiscal discipline and a controlled official exchange-rate path could anchor monthly IPC expectations.
- This supports a forecast with lower monthly inflation by mid/late 2025, while still allowing temporary volatility.

Expected directional effect: lower predicted IPC path.
