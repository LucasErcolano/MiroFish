# Counter-Signal: Dollar Shock And Pass-Through

Evidence favoring an inflation rebound:

- Market chatter around a dollar jump can raise pass-through expectations.
- A widening gap between financial exchange rates and official policy can weaken price-setting confidence.
- Analysts warning about 4-5% monthly inflation imply that disinflation may stall.

Expected directional effect: higher predicted IPC path, especially April/July.
