# Near-Topic Noise: Macro Risk Without Direct IPC Signal

Macro risk context:

- Net reserves pressure, exchange-rate lag, monetary-base movement, and IMF negotiation uncertainty are relevant to the economy.
- These signals matter for the regime but do not directly provide a monthly IPC observation.
- They can distract models into overconfident causal stories without enough numeric support.

Expected directional effect: weak or ambiguous upward pressure.
