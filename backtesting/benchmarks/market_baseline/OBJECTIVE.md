# Objective

Compare MiroFish temporal predictions against market/proxy signals available at each corresponding T package.

The benchmark answers:

1. Does MiroFish improve as evidence accumulates from T0 to T3?
2. Does MiroFish outperform the market/proxy available at that same temporal point?
3. Which signals caused convergence or divergence from the final ground truth?

This is a read-only benchmark over existing artifacts. It does not rerun simulations.
