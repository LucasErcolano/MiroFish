## BASE_03 - Match information

Argentina and Colombia were the finalists of the 2024 CONMEBOL Copa America. The final was scheduled for Sunday, July 14, 2024 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida. It was the first time Argentina and Colombia would decide a Copa America title against each other.

## HIST_01 - Titles and head-to-head context

Argentina entered the final as defending Copa America champion and reigning World Cup champion. Before this final, Argentina had 15 Copa America titles and was tied with Uruguay for the historical record. Colombia had one Copa America title, won at home in 2001.

In Copa America history before the final, Argentina and Colombia had faced each other 15 times: 7 Argentina wins, 3 Colombia wins and 5 draws. Outside group-stage contexts, Argentina was unbeaten against Colombia in five Copa America knockout-stage meetings, including matches decided by penalty shootouts.

## SQUAD_01 - Key player context

The Argentine squad's core included experienced players from recent title cycles, including Lionel Messi, Angel Di Maria, Emiliano Martinez, Cristian Romero, Rodrigo De Paul, Alexis Mac Allister, Enzo Fernandez, Julian Alvarez and Lautaro Martinez. Colombia's core included James Rodriguez, Luis Diaz, Jhon Cordoba, Camilo Vargas, Davinson Sanchez, Jefferson Lerma, Richard Rios and Jhon Arias.

The match profile before adding later evidence can be described structurally as a final between Argentina's recent championship core and Colombia's attempt to convert a strong generation into a second continental title.

## FORM_01 - Argentina semifinal: Argentina 2-0 Canada

Argentina reached the final after beating Canada 2-0 on July 9, 2024. Lionel Messi scored his first goal of the tournament and Julian Alvarez had opened the scoring. The report described Argentina as the tournament favorite and noted that Argentina improved from its quarter-final performance, while still implying that a harder test awaited in the final.

Argentina's semifinal also mattered because Messi played the full 90 minutes after recent concern around an adductor issue. The match report noted that Messi looked brighter and fitter than in the quarter-final against Ecuador, where Argentina had needed Emiliano Martinez and a penalty shootout to advance.

## FORM_02 - Colombia semifinal: Colombia 1-0 Uruguay

Colombia reached the final by beating Uruguay 1-0 on July 10, 2024. Jefferson Lerma scored from a James Rodriguez corner. Colombia played more than half the match with 10 men after Daniel Munoz was sent off, but defended the lead under pressure.

The semifinal showed both Colombia's attacking mechanism and its defensive resilience. James Rodriguez's set-piece delivery created the goal, while Colombia had to protect the lead under sustained Uruguay pressure after the red card. The result sent Colombia to the final against Argentina.

## MARKET_01 - Market odds: Argentina favorite in final markets

Pre-final betting markets listed Argentina as the 90-minute favorite at +105, Colombia as a +360 underdog, and the draw at +190. The same market made Argentina -200 to lift the trophy, with Colombia +170. This implies Argentina was expected to be more likely to win the title, even though the match itself could still be close.

## MARKET_02 - Outright odds after semifinal

Updated Copa America odds after the semifinal had Argentina favored to win the tournament at -170 and Colombia at +130. The same article noted that Argentina reached the final after defeating Canada 2-0 and that Colombia represented a tough physical challenge.

## MARKET_03 - Model and market comparison

FanDuel's pre-final analysis summarized multiple probability signals. It cited an Opta model with Argentina winning in 90 minutes 50.4 percent of the time, a draw at 23.6 percent, and Colombia winning in 90 minutes 25.4 percent of the time. It also cited MasseyRatings with Argentina at 41 percent and Colombia at 31 percent. FanDuel's market price implied Argentina around 47.6 percent to win.

## MODEL_01 - Opta final analysis: Argentina trajectory

Opta's July 13 final analysis framed Argentina as being in a golden era. Argentina was attempting to win three straight major tournaments including Copa America 2021 and World Cup 2022, and could become the first country to win 16 Copa America editions. The same analysis emphasized that Colombia had also reached a potentially historic moment.
