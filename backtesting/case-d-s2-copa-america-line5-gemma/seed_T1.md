## BASE_03 - Match information

Argentina and Colombia were the finalists of the 2024 CONMEBOL Copa America. The final was scheduled for Sunday, July 14, 2024 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida. It was the first time Argentina and Colombia would decide a Copa America title against each other.

## HIST_01 - Titles and head-to-head context

Argentina entered the final as defending Copa America champion and reigning World Cup champion. Before this final, Argentina had 15 Copa America titles and was tied with Uruguay for the historical record. Colombia had one Copa America title, won at home in 2001.

In Copa America history before the final, Argentina and Colombia had faced each other 15 times: 7 Argentina wins, 3 Colombia wins and 5 draws. Outside group-stage contexts, Argentina was unbeaten against Colombia in five Copa America knockout-stage meetings, including matches decided by penalty shootouts.

## SQUAD_01 - Key player context

The Argentine squad's core included experienced players from recent title cycles, including Lionel Messi, Angel Di Maria, Emiliano Martinez, Cristian Romero, Rodrigo De Paul, Alexis Mac Allister, Enzo Fernandez, Julian Alvarez and Lautaro Martinez. Colombia's core included James Rodriguez, Luis Diaz, Jhon Cordoba, Camilo Vargas, Davinson Sanchez, Jefferson Lerma, Richard Rios and Jhon Arias.

The match profile before adding later evidence can be described structurally as a final between Argentina's recent championship core and Colombia's attempt to convert a strong generation into a second continental title.

## FORM_02 - Colombia semifinal: Colombia 1-0 Uruguay

Colombia reached the final by beating Uruguay 1-0 on July 10, 2024. Jefferson Lerma scored from a James Rodriguez corner. Colombia played more than half the match with 10 men after Daniel Munoz was sent off, but defended the lead under pressure.

The semifinal showed both Colombia's attacking mechanism and its defensive resilience. James Rodriguez's set-piece delivery created the goal, while Colombia had to protect the lead under sustained Uruguay pressure after the red card. The result sent Colombia to the final against Argentina.
