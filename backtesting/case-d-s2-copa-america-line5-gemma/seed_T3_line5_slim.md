## COPA_T3_SLIM_01 - Match and historical context

Argentina and Colombia were scheduled to play the 2024 CONMEBOL Copa America final on July 14, 2024 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida. Argentina entered as defending Copa America champion and reigning World Cup champion, with 15 Copa America titles. Colombia had one Copa America title, won in 2001.

Before the final, Argentina and Colombia had met 15 times in Copa America history: 7 Argentina wins, 3 Colombia wins and 5 draws. Argentina was unbeaten against Colombia in five Copa America knockout-stage meetings, including matches decided by penalty shootouts.
