## Extracted context for MiroFish

Argentina and Colombia will play the Grand Final of the CONMEBOL Copa America 2024 on Sunday, July 14, at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida.

Argentina is the defending Copa America champion and one of the most successful teams in the history of the tournament. Argentina is trying to defend its continental title.

Colombia reaches the final with the longest unbeaten streak in its history: 28 matches without defeat, with 22 wins and 6 draws. This surpasses Colombia's previous national unbeaten record.

The final is presented as a meeting between Argentina's title-winning generation and a Colombia team in exceptional form.

Players and tactical factors to consider:

- Argentina has experienced tournament-winning players such as Lionel Messi, Angel Di Maria, Emiliano Martinez, Rodrigo De Paul, Alexis Mac Allister, Lautaro Martinez and Julian Alvarez.
- Colombia has a strong attacking and creative core led by James Rodriguez and Luis Diaz.
- Colombia's confidence is high because of its unbeaten run and strong Copa America campaign.
- Argentina's advantage is its recent record in finals and major tournaments.

Key uncertainty: Colombia's momentum and unbeaten streak could challenge Argentina's experience and favorite status.
